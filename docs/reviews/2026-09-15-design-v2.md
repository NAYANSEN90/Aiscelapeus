## Convergence — found independently by 3+ personas

### C1. The escalation promise is still unkept, and v2 was the document meant to fix it — 6 personas

Verified directly: `dispatch` appears exactly 3 times in 1016 lines (`DESIGN.md:403`, `:495`, `:1010`),
every one a *consumer* of a dispatch system that is never specified. Zero hits for
`notify|paging|on-call|roster|rota|pager|SMS|PSTN` as a mechanism.

It is absent from §4's eight layers, §7's design, §8's 81 requirements, §10's roadmap, and §13's
definition of done. Meanwhile **FR-008 "Clinician bridge" is marked BUILT** (`:730`) with the
criterion *"Bridge requested within 500 ms of the escalation decision; clinician media joinable
within 3 s of accept."*

UX put it best: **"requested" of whom, and "accept" by whom, are the two undefined words carrying
the entire claim.** The criterion is quantitative, testable, and has a named evidence source — it
passes every quality bar §8 sets for itself, while measuring the latency of a message into a void.

And `agent/aiscelapeus/prompts.py:164` still instructs the agent to say
*"A doctor is connecting now, a few more seconds."*

> **Human cost (UX):** a bystander doing compressions on a child stops looking for other help,
> because the machine told them help is arriving. That is the system actively making the outcome
> worse than if it had said nothing.

Field adds the operational half: even with a transport built, there is no roster, no accept SLA,
no no-answer timeout, and no specified agent behaviour during the 90 seconds of waiting.
Systems costs the fix at **4 hours** (webhook POST → audit row → `/doctor` polls a session list).

### C2. The design's own tier discipline does not protect the voice path — 3 personas (Architect, QA, Systems)

§7.9.1 declares a Class-0 call reaching Redis or Qdrant a *build-failing defect*, and RET-R13
asserts `0 spans with retrieval.class == 0 and retrieval.tier != A`.

**The contention it misses is Tier A against itself.** Verified in the vendored SDK: every Moss
operation is `await asyncio.to_thread(...)` — `query` at `moss/client/session_index.py:143`,
`add_docs_text` at `:121`. So §7.9.3's prefetch *writes* and the voice path's Class-0 *reads*
dispatch into the same bounded default executor, and through whatever lock the Rust core holds.

The design's own alert condition **structurally cannot fire on this**, because the offending write
*is* Tier A. RET-R10 and RET-R14 inject latency at Qdrant and Redis — the wrong hop.

Confirmed in current code: `moss_context.py:268-272` holds `self._lock` only for `self._seq += 1`
and releases it before `await session.add_docs(...)`. Benign today with one writer; a genuine
concurrency bug the moment the prefetch worker is added.

**Architect's proposed test (RET-R15)** is the missing one: drive `recall()` at the Class-0 budget
while firing `record_fact()` with deep-chunk-sized payloads from a background task, and measure
read p99 under write load.

- **Nothing injects Moss latency or failure.** The design writes explicit failure rows (§7.9.5) and
  dedicated tests (RET-R11, R28) for Qdrant and Redis — the two stores *forbidden* from the voice

  required on it. `main.py:62` is still a bare unguarded `await context.connect()`.
- **The register is unverifiable until Day 4.** Six FR/NFR rows name `harness` as evidence; the
  harness is Day 4. §10 promises "each day ends with a green headless harness run" for three days
  before the harness exists. There is no test directory, no `conftest.py`, and no `.github/workflows`.
- **Four of the six "tests that carry the design" would pass while the property is violated** (QA):

  a *fake* Redis, so "≤5ms" and "0 PHI" prove only that the fixture was reachable; RET-R12's
  assertion ("folded in **or** discarded") covers both outcomes and cannot fail.
- **NFR-002 measures "final transcript → first TTS byte"** — a definition excluding the two vendor
  legs most likely to blow the budget on stage. Zero tests inject Deepgram or ElevenLabs failure.

## Highest-severity single findings

### F1. Up to 10 seconds of silence mid-CPR (Field) — PROD blocker

`DESIGN.md:243-248`, verified: the state machine has **no `FULL → OFFLINE` edge**. A clean blackout
must serialize 3s (DEGRADED sustain) + 5s (OFFLINE trigger) + 2s (OFF-004) = **~10 seconds**, or
roughly 18–20 compressions delivered with no cadence and no explanation.

> A lay rescuer who hears nothing at second four assumes the phone is dead, stops, and looks at it.
> Stopping compressions is the failure mode that kills.

Two individually-reasonable thresholds compose serially because of a missing edge in a diagram.

A second composition bug on the recovery path: `OFFLINE → DEGRADED` requires *"queue drained"*, so a
responder whose network returns stays in offline behaviour until an arbitrarily large audio upload
completes.

**And OFF-001's acceptance criterion — "0 flaps across a 10-minute degrading-network simulation" —
is satisfiable by a machine that never transitions at all.** Real cellular oscillates: 2–8s dropouts
on a 20–60s period are each *shorter* than the 5s trigger, so the system parks in DEGRADED forever
and never arms the local corpus.

### F2. The gate's rejection line misattributes a software failure to the patient (UX) — PROD blocker

`DESIGN.md:468`: *"Stay with me. I need a clinician for this one, bringing them in now."*

Spoken when a Pydantic model rejected a numeric that did not appear verbatim in a retrieved card.
The responder's belief updates about the *patient*; the truth is about the *schema*. At R3's <5%
target that is roughly once per 20-turn call, with no back-off and no different line on repeat.

UX's fix: name the system as the failing party and do not stop the responder —
*"Keep going, same rate. I need to check that with a clinician."* And amend CONFLICT-1: the right
trade is **re-speak the last verified instruction** plus a quiet flag, not a generic alarm.

### F3. The §7.1 sampler is not something the OTel SDK can do (Systems) — DEMO blocker

`opentelemetry/sdk/trace/sampling.py:192-201`: `should_sample()` is called at span start and must
return immediately. A decision depending on what happens later is **tail sampling**, which lives in
the Collector — a seventh container. Building it in-process means an unbounded in-memory span buffer
on a voice agent, contradicting §7.1's own "export never touches the voice path."

OBS-007 is a 2–3 day job, or a 20-minute one if honestly restated as "100% sampling for the demo."

### F4. Act 2.5 is scripted around a component the cut list pre-commits to deleting (Marketing)

§11's Act 2.5 asks the audience to register that a pediatric variant returned *"in single-digit
milliseconds because the previous scenario warmed it"* — an **inaudible** differential. §10 cut item
6 pre-commits to cutting Redis.

Architect adds the deeper point: the card schema at `DESIGN.md:598` already carries
`variants: {pediatric: card-cpr-pediatric, ...}` — a **Tier-A, in-process pointer**. The demo's
showcase beat for the two-tier architecture is the one case the *single*-tier design already handles,
and `:639` ("the deep document is authoritative for variants") directly contradicts `:598`.

## Where the personas disagree

**Should ADR-007/008 (Qdrant + Redis) be cut?**

- **Architect: delete both now.** At a 14KB / 13-category corpus, ADR-007's four justifications are
  premature. Delete them and the responder still gets ≤10ms cited card guidance. Appendix B Q6 admits
  the card/deep boundary is "a guess" — the design commits three datastores, 30 test cases, two
  fixtures and a cancellation protocol *before* answering the question that determines whether the
  split should exist.
- **Systems: cut entirely, recover 2.5–3 days.** Keep the ADR text as stated future architecture.
- **Marketing: keep in the doc, cut from the pitch.** ADR-001 is the best paragraph in the document;
  ADR-007/008 subordinate it to a footnote about tiers. Three datastores at a *Moss* sprint dilutes.
- **Field: cut Redis and the planner rows first** (§10 items 6–7 are pure online polish with a
  documented degrade path) and spend that Day-4 time shipping deep sections for the top 5–6 protocols
  into the **offline** bundle.
- **PM: move the whole subsystem to a "designed, not built" appendix** with its test plan attached.

**Field's priority inversion is the strongest argument in the review:**

> Day 4's Qdrant/Redis work makes the *online* responder's guidance richer — and the online responder
> is the one who can already reach a human. The offline responder (basement, rural road, disaster) is
> statistically furthest from help, needs contraindications and variants most, and has their retrieval
> quality sitting at cut-list item 3.

## What the design gets right

Unanimous across personas, and worth protecting:

- **§3's self-audit is verified-accurate and rare.** It checks the repo, not the PRD, and names the
  README's own correlation-ID claim as probably false. I spot-checked it: the FastAPI absence, the
  zero `correlation_id` hits, the `/api/token` role-from-body defect (`route.ts:46`), and
  CONFLICT-2's `SimpleSpanProcessor` (`telemetry.py:88`) are all correct. Marketing calls it the most
  commercially valuable asset in the document.
- **ADR-001 is the actual pitch.** "Moss is the architecture, not a dependency," proved by a
  *deletion*. Architect and Marketing agree it is the strongest framing available at this sprint.
- **ADR-004's deterministic ratchet is the real differentiator** (Marketing). Sub-10ms Moss retrieval
  is table stakes at a Moss sprint; a rule that outranks the LLM is a property almost nobody else will
  have — because it requires deciding the model is not trustworthy.
- **CONFLICT-3 is the most intellectually honest paragraph** (Systems, Marketing, QA). It refuses the
  mentor's literal AES-256 ask, explains why searchable encryption cannot run in 10ms, and substitutes
  pseudonymisation-at-write.
- **The no-blend rule (§7.9.4, RET-R50)** — on a numeric disagreement the agent speaks *neither*
  number and escalates. Correct behaviour toward someone who will act on whatever they hear.
- **The cut list is designed for removability** (Systems, Architect): item 6 keeps the `cache.lookup`
  span recording every call as a miss "so the metric still reads truthfully." That instruction only
  comes from someone who has actually cut a subsystem at 23:00.
- **The Moss API surface is real** (Systems, verified): `MossClient.session()`, `load_index`,
  `push_index` all exist and are used correctly. `@moss-dev/moss-web` genuinely exists (v1.0.1,
  2026-07-21) — though single-maintainer and 8 weeks old, which makes ADR-006's Plan B the right hedge.
- **Measured, not guessed** (Systems): NFR-003 at 14 spans × 15 attributes → **p99 1.91ms** against a
  5ms budget. Achievable — but max already touches 5.04ms, and this excludes attribute construction
  (2–3× real).

## Recommended next actions, in order

Six of seven personas nominated a different "next commit." They are not in conflict — they are a
sequence. Ordered by (safety × cheapness) ÷ dependency:

1. **Write §7.10 "Escalation delivery" and add an acknowledgement state that gates the agent's
   speech** (UX, ~4h per Systems). Until acknowledged, the agent must never say "a doctor is
   connecting" — it says *"I've called for a doctor. Keep going — I'll tell you the second they're
   on."* This single state variable fixes the largest safety defect, makes FR-008's "accept" mean
   something, gives FR-009's 2s clock an event to start from, and supplies the honest §7.4 rejection
   line. Amend `prompts.py:164` in the same commit.
2. **Fix the trace spine before any Docker** (Systems, <40 lines, no containers, no credentials).
   `BatchSpanProcessor` at `telemetry.py:88`, then open a `session.root` span in `main.py:57` and
   attach it via a `contextvars.ContextVar` inside `span()`. Today every `span()` from a
   `@function_tool()` body is its own trace root — OBS-001 fails now and would keep failing through
   every later feature. It is the precondition for every OBS-*, NFR-003 and RET-R80 assertion.
3. **Add the `FULL → OFFLINE` edge and pre-arm the bundle on DEGRADED entry** (Field). Cut the
   OFFLINE trigger to ~1.5s, speak a pre-rendered bridge line within 500ms, start a local metronome.
   Rewrite OFF-001 against an *oscillating* profile (5s down / 15s up, repeated), asserting continuous
   guidance rather than "0 flaps."
4. **Write `agent/tests/test_triage.py`** (QA, ~60 lines, no network). The one component ADR-004 calls
   "the single most defensible safety property" has no requirement-level test anywhere in §8. Turn the
   prior review's concrete misses into a parametrised table, add the ratchet property test, wire
   `pytest` to `.github/workflows/`. This gives Days 1–3 the acceptance mechanism they currently lack.
5. **Correct the register before it becomes PRD v2** (PM, Systems). FR-003, FR-007, FR-008, OBS-003,
   OBS-004 → PARTIAL or NEW. Carrying a false BUILT in front of a clinically literate judge costs more
   than the gap it hides — and a wrong status silently removes work from the roadmap.
6. **Re-cut the roadmap to real capacity** (PM, Architect, Systems, Field converge here). Move §7.9 in
   full — ADR-007/008, RET-001…014, RET-R01…R80, the corpus pipeline — into a "designed, not built"
   appendix. Recovers Day 2's largest item and ~80% of Day 4. Then invert Field's priority: spend the
   recovered time on the offline bundle's depth, not the online cache.
7. **Re-cut §2 and §11 to one claim** (Marketing). Lead with model-independent auditable escalation;
   demote latency and traceability to its supports. Fold the pediatric switch into Act 1 as a
   *correctness* beat with no mention of caching. Cut Act 3 from 5 minutes to 3 (keep the explainability
   panel and the chain tamper; move Phoenix trees and SOAP to Q&A). Add a 60-second honest close —
   "not a medical device" currently appears once, in a risk-register cell, pointing at a README no
   judge opens.

   document at a known revision, every numeric verbatim, CI-enforced" is something a weekend competitor
   cannot say.
3. **Moss usage is still zero** (`DESIGN.md:89`), on a Moss-sponsored sprint, with the subscription set
   to cancel. R2 mitigates cancellation but not the usage number; first meaningful load is Day 4.
4. **Consent appears zero times in 1016 lines** — against 7-year PHI retention of a patient who is by
   definition unconscious, recorded by a bystander never told the call is retained. SEC-012 promises a
   GDPR mapping; Article 9 is not addressed.
