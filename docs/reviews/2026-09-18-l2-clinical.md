**Date:** 18 Sept 2026
**Reviewer:** `readiness-field` persona, adversarial, read-only
**Subject:** `agent/aiscelapeus/assessment.py` (new), plus `triage.py` and `escalation.py`
**Scope:** clinical only — is the medicine right, and right *for this product*
**Outcome:** five findings. Fixed before commit; no L2 code was ever committed unreviewed.

| Bar | Score |
|---|---|

| PROD | **2/5** |

> **Three reachable paths gave a bystander *with the correct information* the wrong next
> action.** That is the distinguishing feature of these findings: they are not gaps where
> the machine lacks data, they are branches taken on complete inputs.

Every finding below was **reproduced by execution** before being acted on.

## Blocker 1 — catastrophic haemorrhage deferred behind questions · **BOTH BARS**

`assessment.py:763` placed the C block after the responsiveness and breathing gates.

```
scene_safe=True, responsiveness=ALERT, severe_bleeding=PRESENT, breathing=UNKNOWN
    -> ASK_BREATHING_QUALITY
scene_safe=True, severe_bleeding=PRESENT (rest unknown)
    -> ASK_RESPONSIVENESS
```

A bystander kneeling on a femoral bleed is asked *"is he breathing normally, or are there
occasional noisy gasping breaths?"* — so they take their hands off the wound to look at the
chest, or they answer and wait. **Exsanguination is measured in 2–3 minutes**, and the airway
of a talking patient is self-evidently patent. This is precisely why the **C-ABC / MARCH**
reordering exists.

The fix is structural rather than a special case: a pre-A gate on an *established* bleed, in
the same position as the scene gate. The machine already proved it can do this — the scene
gate is exactly that shape.

## Blocker 2 — a bleeding arrest is told to compress and never told to stop the blood · **BOTH BARS**

`_arrest_branch` returns before the C block is evaluated and reads `severe_bleeding` nowhere.

```
breathing=ABNORMAL_OR_GASPING, severe_bleeding=PRESENT  ->  INSTRUCT_CPR
INSTRUCT_CONTROL_BLEEDING unreachable from that state
```

*"Arrest outranks a limb bleed"* is right as a priority and **wrong for traumatic arrest**:
haemorrhage control is *part of* the resuscitation, not a later letter. Compressions on an
uncontrolled arterial bleed pump the remaining circulating volume onto the road — the one
situation where CPR is known to be futile without controlling the source.

The single-rescuer conflict is genuine: you cannot pack a wound and compress at once. **That
is exactly why it must be resolved in code rather than left for the LLM to improvise.**
Silence is the worst option, because the bystander receives compressions-only guidance with
no mention of the blood.

## Blocker 3 — the scene gate is an absorbing state · **PROD**

**The gate itself is correct and stays.** DR before ABC; a rescuer electrocuted across a
downed cable produces two dead people; one turn is the right price. Three implementation
faults, one of which kills:

1. **No escape.** `scene_safe=False` never advances — verified with a full arrest behind it.
   A caller already at the patient's side in a lay-by who answers *"no, cars are going past"*
   **truthfully** is told to move to safety, forever. **The gate kills the patient it was
   meant to protect, precisely when the bystander has already taken the risk and is not going
   to un-take it.** A real dispatcher asks *"are you safe where you are right now?"* and moves
   on; a persisting hazard gets a live warning, not a blocking loop.
2. **An arrest behind an unresolved scene asked for `SEVERE` (4), not `CRITICAL` (5).** The
   gate must not lower the criticality of a known arrest.
3. **The question's phrasing is unpinned.** `question_key="scene_safe"` leaves the one gate
   between a reported arrest and compressions to be phrased by the non-deterministic layer.
   *"Is the scene safe?"* invites a freeze (*"I don't know… I think so?"*) or a reflexive
   *"yes"* carrying no information. The answerable form is a **closed hazard list** — traffic,
   fire, electricity, yes or no — and unlike the breathing question, nothing pinned it.

## Blocker 4 — recovery position with no mechanism-of-injury input · **PROD**

Fires on unconscious + breathing normal + no bleed, and `AssessmentInputs` holds no
spinal/mechanism field — so **the unconscious motorcyclist and the unconscious fainter produce
an identical unconditional imperative.**

The DRY argument for keeping the caveat's *wording* in `protocols.py` is accepted. What is not
accepted is the clinical consequence: the caveat is a **conditional**, its condition is an
input L2 does not hold, and whether it survives into speech therefore depends on retrieval
landing the right document at 64 % top-1. On a one-in-three miss a bystander rolls an unstable
C-spine.

Airway still beats spine. The point is **which technique is named** — jaw thrust with manual
in-line stabilisation versus a full roll, and only one of those is safe to improvise.

## Blocker 5 — two dead steps, and choking has no representation · **PROD**

`ASK_AGE_BAND` and `INSTRUCT_OPEN_AIRWAY` are declared and never returned; `airway_opened` is
an input nothing reads. Verified by AST: 11 step uses, neither among them.

`INSTRUCT_OPEN_AIRWAY` is **not** reachable in a state where it is wrong — because it is not
reachable at all. Safe today, **misleading tomorrow**, since a future author sees a member and
a matching input field and assumes a designed slot.

The clinical hole it leaves: **no choking path at all.** A witnessed choking collapse arrives
as `ABNORMAL_OR_GASPING` or `NONE` and routes straight to `INSTRUCT_CPR`. Compressions will not
shift a lodged bolus, and back blows / abdominal thrusts in a still-conscious choker is the
intervention with the survival curve. For an untrained bystander with one patient this is a
**common, fast, reversible** death and the machine had one answer for it and it was wrong.

## The four removals — three right, one loses something

| Removal | Verdict |
|---|---|
| JumpSTART's pulse gate on the 5 breaths | **Right**, and for a stronger reason than the code gave: it is an MCI rationing step imported without its preconditions. The code's "it could not have changed the outcome anyway" argument makes the removal look like a refactor. Pulse stays recordable for the bridge — the right disposition. |
| Capillary refill | **Right.** Needs a timed 5 s press at heart level in good light in a warm environment; a fabricated answer is worse than UNKNOWN. **But it loses the compensated-shock patient** — pale, clammy, tachycardic, still talking — who now has no discriminator and exits at Criticality 2. The replacement is not cap refill, it is the layperson-observable cluster: cold sweaty skin, grey or ashen, *"he's gone white"*, drowsiness, thirst. |
| Counted respiratory rate | **Right, and the three-state replacement is an upgrade rather than a loss.** RR 28 vs 34 straddles START's RED boundary and no bystander can resolve it. The three states capture the only respiratory distinction that changes a bystander's action. |
| START step 1 "can they walk?" | **Right, and loses nothing for one patient.** The walking check is a *sorting* device whose entire output is "this person is GREEN, move on" — a decision that only exists with a queue. The harm is real: *"see if he can stand"* to a possible spine or pelvic injury. The rapid "this one is fine" read is not lost: ALERT + NORMAL + no bleed reaches `INSTRUCT_MONITOR` in the same number of turns. |

## Provenance as medicine — the asymmetry holds

Three adversarial cases tried, none broke it. **The caller who recants** (*"actually I think
he is breathing"*) is a report, so it arrives as evidence, and evidence-lowering-evidence is
refused — the recant cannot stand the incident down. **The bystander who wants the ambulance
cancelled** has no path at all. The explicit `provenance=EVIDENCE` in `escalation.py` is the
load-bearing line: the default `ASSUMPTION` there would have let L2 arithmetic stand down a
reported arrest.

**One known limitation, recorded rather than treated as complete:** a patient who *genuinely
improves* — post-ictal, hypoglycaemic after sugar, resolved faint — has no expression in this
model, since improvement is evidence lowering evidence. The right default for a field agent
(the ratchet exists so the record survives a calm five minutes), but the criticality number
stops tracking the patient after the first evidenced peak, and **the clinician on the bridge
has no channel to resolve it.**

## Strengths, in the reviewer's own ranking

- **The arrest path is safe structurally, not by convention.** `_arrest_branch` is returned to
  before any other consideration, has no leaf below `CRITICAL`, and reads `inputs.pulse`
  nowhere. The exhaustive walk over ~30 000 input combinations
  (`test_no_reachable_branch_withholds_resuscitation_from_an_arrest`) asserts it with an
  explicit whitelist of permitted precursors rather than a `continue` that would excuse a
  wrong branch. **The strongest safety evidence in this repo.**
- **`Breathing.routes_to_cpr` as a property of the value** makes `if breathing is
  Breathing.NONE` an expression with nowhere to be correct — which is what stops Blocker 3
  being reintroduced by the next author's convenience check.
- **`ASSUMPTION` by default in `triage.py`** means the dangerous direction — a guess
  overturning a report — is closed by construction rather than by review discipline.
- **"An assumption anywhere on the path makes the whole branch an assumption"** is the
  clinically conservative reading, and it makes correctability decidable from the branch alone.

# Round 2 — re-review of the blocker fixes

**Date:** 18 Sept 2026
**Outcome:** four of five blockers convincingly closed. **PROD still 2/5, for different reasons.**

| Bar | Round 1 | Round 2 |
|---|---|---|

## Confirmed fixed

- **Blocker 2's fix is the best clinical work in the module.** `INSTRUCT_CONTROL_BLEEDING_THEN_CPR`
  names three hands-free options in increasing order of effectiveness and decreasing
  availability — knee, body weight, tourniquet over packing. *"Kneeling on a groin or axillary
  bleed is how a lone medic buys hands"*, and body weight through a knee delivers more force
  than an untrained person's arms will sustain. **"Do both; do not choose" is the single best
  line in the file.** No change requested.
- **Blocker 3 is not reintroduced by the awake-gasping fix.** Verified: `PAIN` and
  `UNRESPONSIVE` + gasping both return `INSTRUCT_CPR` at CRITICAL with no gate, and
  `is_unconscious` correctly includes P as well as U.
- **The choking scope is right for the right reason, not luck.** Back blows rely on an upright
  patient and an assisted cough; the collapsed choker reaching compressions is current guidance.
- **`ASK_AIRWAY_OBSTRUCTION` is the best-written question in the module** — pairing *"did
  somebody SEE this start"* with *"can he answer you?"* does double duty, discriminating
  choking **and** silently re-testing a possibly-wrong "he's alert" report.

## New defects — all reproduced by execution

### 1. The awake-gasping fix is one notch too wide, and the suite PINS the defect

`routes_to_cpr` gates on responsiveness for **both** non-normal states, including `NONE`:

```
scene=SAFE, responsiveness=ALERT, airway_obstruction=NONE, breathing=NONE
  -> INSTRUCT_SUPPORT_SEVERE_BREATHING_DIFFICULTY, Criticality 4
  "Do NOT start compressions... Sit them upright... use their OWN reliever inhaler."
```

**To a patient reported as not breathing at all.** The conjunction was imported faithfully, but
the failure it guards — compressions on a talking asthmatic — only ever existed for
`ABNORMAL_OR_GASPING`. Apnoea in a patient still called "alert" is a **stale or wrong
responsiveness report**, the most likely error in the first sixty seconds of a panicking call.
*The correct handling of a contradiction between "alert" and "not breathing" is to re-test the
contradiction, not to resolve it silently in favour of the reassuring half.*

**Worse than a latent hole:** `test_no_reachable_branch_compresses_the_chest_of_an_awake_patient`
walks the awake space demanding no compressions anywhere in it. A future author who spots the
apnoea case breaks two named tests and concludes *they* are wrong. The repo's recurring failure
mode inverted — not a vacuous test, a test that **defends a defect**.

### 2. The distress leaf breaks the provenance contract

The only instruction leaf calling neither `inputs.assumed(...)` nor passing `assumed=`. Reached
with `scene_safe` at ASSUMED_WORST it reports `provenance=EVIDENCE`, `assumed_inputs=()` —
making its Criticality 4 **uncorrectable by later evidence**, the exact inversion ASM-14 exists
to prevent, on the leaf whose patient is most likely to change state.

### 3. The arrest path discards a witnessed foreign body

`_arrest_branch` never reads `airway_obstruction`. A bystander who watched food go in and the
patient collapse is never told to look in the mouth — current guidance is compressions **plus**
checking for a now-displaced visible object before ventilations (never a blind sweep).
**Blocker 2's defect shape exactly** — an established finding the arrest path did not read —
fixed for bleeding and not for choking.

### 4. The anaphylaxis positioning contradicts the corpus

The leaf says *"Sit them upright... do NOT lay them flat"*, unconditionally and in capitals.
`protocols.py:anaphylaxis` says *"Lay them flat with legs raised; sit them up only if breathing
is hard."* Both defensible — flat-with-legs-raised exists because anaphylaxis patients who stand
up arrest (the empty-ventricle phenomenon) — but **L2 outranks retrieval**, so the unconditional
imperative wins and loses the one positioning rule that has killed people. Asthma, pulmonary
oedema and a stabbed chest reach the same leaf, so any fix must be right for all four.

## Contested — recorded rather than silently resolved

**The spinal leaf coaches jaw thrust + manual in-line stabilisation, and Round 2 judges that
wrong for an untrained operator.** It is a two-handed clinician technique performed from above
the head; the untrained failure mode is pressure on the submandibular soft tissue, which pushes
the tongue *into* the airway. It commits both hands permanently to a patient who also needs
watching for arrest — in a module that elsewhere goes out of its way to name hands-free methods.
Modern lay guidance puts a suspected-spine unconscious patient in the recovery position with the
head supported and the body turned as a unit.

It also collides with `UNPERFORMABLE_ASSESSMENTS`' own principle: *a jaw thrust is a fabricated
airway* — the bystander reports they are holding it and the machine cannot know it is not
working. **Round 1 asked for the distinction; Round 2 says the wrong half was called safe to
improvise.** Both positions are on record.

## Further gaps recorded

- **`SceneSafety` cannot distinguish a hazard you can work beside from a hazard that is IN the
  patient.** Live electricity makes touching the patient the mechanism of injury — CPR on a
  patient still in circuit electrocutes the rescuer. Fire and gas are dose-over-time. The pinned
  question already enumerates traffic/fire/electricity, so the information is collected and then
  **discarded into a two-value verdict** — the same arity defect the enum was widened to fix,
  one level down.
- **The committed-unsafe escape hatch is unreachable by the pinned phrasing.** It appears only
  in the `INSTRUCT_MAKE_SCENE_SAFE` rationale, so the only route is to be told to leave and then
  push back. *"The escape hatch exists but the door is behind the wall."*
- **`_hazard_suffix` is a constant string appended indefinitely** — identical repetition is the
  definition of an alarm that gets tuned out, and L2 never holds *which* hazard it was.
- **No "bleeding controlled" input**, so an established bleed returns `INSTRUCT_CONTROL_BLEEDING`
  forever — the absorbing-loop shape the scene-gate fix killed, recurring.
- **Every finding is currently latent**: `decide` has no production caller. Which is the right
  time to fix them.

# Round 3 — verdict: DO NOT COMMIT

**Date:** 18 Sept 2026 · **DEMO 4/5 · PROD 2/5** — unchanged for a third round, for new reasons.

## Confirmed genuinely fixed

- **The apnoea routing is correct at BOTH the category and the instruction.** `Breathing.NONE`
  reaches the arrest path from every responsiveness value including ALERT, and `_is_arrest`
  carries the same asymmetry so the choking gate no longer caps a reported arrest at SEVERE.
- **Question-outranks-arrest-route is "the subtlest decision in the file" and right.**
  `ASK_AIRWAY_OBSTRUCTION` wins because it *decides* the route, and `_reaches_choking_block`
  holds that reading in one predicate consulted by both sites, so the two cannot drift.
- **`_mouth_check_clause` is correctly scoped**, with the blind-sweep prohibition as a
  precondition rather than advice, and the tune-out guard is real.
- **All 72 unresponsive/PAIN cells resuscitate**; no cell in 216 withholds resuscitation from
  an established arrest. *"That half of the fix chain is finished."*

## Blocker 1 — the bleed hoist outranks the airway on a patient who has no airway

The coordinator's candidate defect, **confirmed real**:

```
ALERT + breathing NONE + WITNESSED_FOREIGN_BODY + bleed PRESENT -> INSTRUCT_CONTROL_BLEEDING
ALERT + breathing NONE + obstruction UNKNOWN    + bleed PRESENT -> same, question never asked
```

The spoken rationale ends *"keep the hands on the wound while answering anything else"* — which
**actively instructs the bystander not to act on the airway** while a conscious person with a
lodged bolus and zero air movement arrests in front of them.

**Why round 2's recorded judgement does not cover it.** The pinned test builds its case with
`breathing` left **UNKNOWN** and justifies the priority with *"a patient who is STILL RESPONSIVE
is by definition still moving some air."* Sound for an unestablished value — Blocker 1's case —
and **false** for `breathing` established as `NONE`. The gate's own spoken words make the same
unqualified claim: *"a patient who can be asked about is a patient with an airway."* True of a
talking patient, false of this one. **The judgement was applied outside the premise that
justified it**, and no test pinned the established-NONE variant.

**Performability is settled, and the module already knows how.**
`INSTRUCT_CONTROL_BLEEDING_THEN_CPR` solves this exact single-rescuer conflict —
*"your knee, your weight, or a tight bandage or tourniquet over packing"* frees both hands, and
thrusts need both hands. **A forced choice is not honest here, because the module has already
proved the choice is avoidable for the unresponsive twin of this patient.**

## Blocker 2 — the guard test commissioned to find the provenance defect cannot find it

```python
checked += 1
if name not in branch.assumed_inputs:
    continue                                    # <- skips the defect
assert branch.provenance is Provenance.ASSUMPTION
```

A leaf that **correctly records** an input is asserted. A leaf that **fails to record** it is
skipped. The test can only ever fire on leaves that already got it right — and its own comment
three lines above claims *"Any leaf that reads an input without recording it fails here."*

**Proven by inverting it**: `continue` → `assert name in branch.assumed_inputs` immediately
fails with `ASK_RESPONSIVENESS read scene_safe on its path but did not record it as assumed`,
so the defect is wider than the two leaves reported.

> This is now the **fifth** instance of one pattern in this repo: the vacuous CI job, the
> `_no_real_dotenv` fixture that only `yield`ed, the test that *defended* the apnoea defect,
> the `DIAGNOSIS_PATTERNS` rule unreachable through its caller, and now a guard whose comment
> asserts a property it does not have. **A mechanism whose only implementation is its own
> description.** Worth treating as this project's signature failure mode.

## Blockers 3–5 — separate scoped changes, explicitly not to be bundled

- **Live electricity must be re-weighted, not merely recorded.** It was *latent* while `UNSAFE`
  blocked; the three-state fix made it **reachable all the way to `INSTRUCT_CPR`**.
  `_hazard_suffix` says *"keep them watching for it… get clear if it closes in"* — which
  describes a hazard that moves toward you, **not one you are about to place both hands on**.
  A bystander who said *"there's a live cable, I'm already next to him"* is told to compress.
  Two casualties instead of one — the outcome the DR gate exists to prevent.
- **`_contradiction_clause`'s routing is right and its wording is wrong.** *"Note the
  contradiction"*, *"cannot both be true"*, *"tell them what they told you"* instruct the
  operator to relitigate the caller's report mid-compression. **A real dispatcher never tells a
  caller they contradicted themselves — they re-test invisibly**, which this repo already knows
  how to do: `ASK_AIRWAY_OBSTRUCTION` re-tests the same wrong "he's alert" report with *"can he
  answer you?"*. *"Do not stop compressions" is a mitigation bolted onto a sentence that invites
  the stop.*
- **The deterioration rule is wrong for pulmonary oedema.** The greyness and clamminess *are*
  the cardiogenic failure, and flat-with-legs-raised increases venous return into a failing left
  ventricle and worsens the oedema — the one presentation where the fainting-position reflex is
  directly harmful, in exactly this awake-and-breathing-badly population. The recorded gap
  concedes the tension-pneumothorax objection for the stab wound and **does not address
  pulmonary oedema, where the mechanism argument runs the other way.**

## Verification limit, stated rather than asserted

The reviewer judged the spoken rationales by reading them and did not execute the L3 or
`protocols.py` layer, so **what a bystander finally hears after retrieval and phrasing is
unverified** — only what L2 hands over.
